# Storefront Privacy and Retention Policy

This policy applies to each published Candle Manager storefront. Each store owner is responsible for publishing contact information and having this policy reviewed for the jurisdiction where they operate before production use.

## Customer accounts and addresses

- Customer names, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, preferences, and saved addresses are used only to operate the storefront account, fulfill orders, and send communications the customer opted into.
- Passwords are stored as password hashes. Customer sign-in sessions expire after 30 days and are removed when the customer deletes their account.
- Saved account addresses are kept while the account is active. Deleting the account removes active account access, sign-in sessions, and saved addresses immediately.

## Orders and payments

- Completed order snapshots, including shipping details, order amounts, and payment-provider references, are retained for seven years for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, chargeback, and customer-service obligations.
- Deleted customer accounts are anonymized. Historical orders remain as required records and are no longer connected to an active customer login.
- Card numbers, wallet credentials, and PayPal credentials are never stored in Candle Manager. Payment providers handle the payment instruments.

## Uploaded media and future customer content

- Label artwork and customer review photos must be limited to the stated purpose of the associated order or review.
- When those features are enabled, unapproved, rejected, or abandoned uploads must be removed within 90 days. Approved content remains only while the related product, order, or review is active, then is deleted within 90 days unless a legal hold applies.

## Customer choices

- Marketing and reminder messages require opt-in and can be turned off in the customer account.
- Customers can delete their active account from the account area. Store owners must provide a contact method for requests about retained order records or applicable privacy rights.
