Important current state
-----------------------
- The public storefront has a product catalog and a browser-local cart.
- Public checkout creates customer order records, reserves stock, and delegates payment capture to configured Square or PayPal providers.

Phase 0 - Architecture decisions and safeguards
------------------------------------------------
[x] Confirm which storefront capabilities require Elite, and which should be available on lower tiers. Policy: all storefront publishing, customer accounts, customer checkout, orders, and storefront-commerce tools require Elite. Lower tiers retain internal production tools only.
[x] Define shipping regions, local pickup rules, sales-tax responsibility, and refund/cancellation policy for store owners. Initial policy: United States shipping only, no local pickup until scheduling is implemented, no automatic tax calculation, and owner-managed cancellation/refund handling. Non-US checkout addresses are rejected server-side.
[x] Define product types: physical candle, sample, bundle, custom-made, subscription, gift card, and service/booking. Product inventory now stores and validates a product type, with physical as the safe default.
[x] Define order states: draft, awaiting payment, paid, in production, ready for pickup, shipped, delivered, cancelled, refunded. Checkout creates awaiting-payment orders; the owner queue now manages the complete lifecycle, including refunds and cancellations.
[x] Define inventory rules: checkout reserves available stock for 30 minutes, expired unpaid reservations are automatically cancelled, each checkout checks stock minus active reservations, and successful provider payment atomically deducts stock. Owner actions cannot mark an order paid or move unpaid orders into fulfillment, preventing manual oversells.
[x] Define data-retention and privacy rules for customer accounts, addresses, uploaded label artwork, and review photos. The public customer account now shows the policy and supports deleting the active profile, sessions, and saved addresses. Completed-order snapshots remain for seven years; future abandoned or rejected label/review uploads must be purged within 90 days. See docs/STOREFRONT_PRIVACY_RETENTION.md.
[x] Do not store raw card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal credentials in the app database. Payment instruments and temporary checkout tokens are never persisted; only provider-generated transaction references, status, amount, currency, and timestamps are stored after payment. Public customer responses do not expose provider references. See docs/PAYMENT_DATA_HANDLING.md.
