The features I would prioritize

For your site, I think the strongest structure would be:

1. Create Your Own Candle
Pick size → scent → wick → label → extras.

2. Create Your Own Collection
Pick 3, 4, 6, or 12 candles and name the collection.

3. Discovery Packs
Small samples that lead customers toward full-size purchases.

4. Candle Subscription
A predictable recurring-revenue product.

5. Gift Builder
Very useful around Christmas, Valentine’s Day, Mother’s Day, birthdays, housewarmings, etc.

6. Scent Quiz + Scent Finder
Makes a large scent catalog much easier to shop.

7. Limited Edition Drops
Creates urgency instead of having every scent available forever.

8. Rewards + Referrals
Helps turn one-time buyers into repeat customers.

9. Custom/Event Orders
This could become a surprisingly large revenue source.

10. Build-a-Box
Something like:

Choose your box
→ Pick 4 candles
→ Pick 4 scents
→ Pick your labels
→ Add samples
→ Add gift message
→ Checkout
